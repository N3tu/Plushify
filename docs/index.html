Into the game

  1. Pick a skin

    Upload your PNG and check the preview. Switch between Classic and Slim if needed.

  2. Install the pack

    In Minecraft Java, open Options › Resource Packs › Open Pack Folder. Drop the ZIP into that folder and activate it.

  3. Equip armor

    Rename your Totem at an anvil to Iron, Gold, Diamond, Netherite, Copper or Chain.

Install in Modrinth

  1. Download your Plushy ZIP.
  2. Open your instance in the Modrinth App and select Content.
  3. Drag the downloaded ZIP into the content list.
  4. Enable Plushify under Options › Resource Packs in Minecraft.
