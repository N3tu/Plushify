Privacy

Plushify is maintained by N3TU and hosted on GitHub Pages.

Skin previews and downloads

Skin previews and resource-pack ZIPs are generated in your browser. In private mode, your skin stays on your device. Reloading the creator clears the selected skin. Downloaded files remain wherever you save them.

Website hosting

Plushify does not set cookies or run analytics. Your browser requests the page and assets from GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP addresses of Pages visitors for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its handling of service data.

Public skin gallery

Public sharing is optional. If you allow it, valid skins you upload afterward are automatically published. Anyone can view and download them. Only share skins you have permission to publish. Your browser saves your choice locally. Change it in Sharing settings for future uploads.

The gallery receives the skin’s pixels and stores a generated PNG, an image identifier and the time it was first published. The gallery database does not store your original filename, account or IP address. Cloudflare serves the gallery and processes request data, including IP addresses used for upload rate limits. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Contact

Contact N3TU to ask a question or request a skin’s removal. Include the skin’s download link and avoid posting personal information publicly. Copies already downloaded by others may remain after removal.
